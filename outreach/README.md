# Outreach

Drafts of messages that go out under Alam's name (tasks 1.1, 1.2, and replies to reviewers in 6.4). Claude writes the draft here; Alam edits and sends it from his own account.

The repo is public, so replies stay out of it. Keep each reply in a private place (your mail, or the claude.ai project) and paste it into a session when Claude needs it. In the draft's "Reply" section, record only the facts you may cite, with the date. Ask the sender before quoting a reply in the paper or here.

Each file: `<task-id>_<recipient>.md` with To, Subject, Body, the date sent, and the citable facts from the reply.
