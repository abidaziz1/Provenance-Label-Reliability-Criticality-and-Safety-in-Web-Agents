# Outreach

Drafts of messages that go out under Abid Aziz's name (tasks 1.1, 1.2, and replies to reviewers in 6.4). Abid Aziz reviews and sends drafts from his own account.

The repo is public, so replies stay out of it. Keep each reply in a private place (your private mail archive) and share it only with authorized collaborators. In the draft's "Reply" section, record only the facts you may cite, with the date. Ask the sender before quoting a reply in the paper or here.

Each file: `<task-id>_<recipient>.md` with To, Subject, Body, the date sent, and the citable facts from the reply.
