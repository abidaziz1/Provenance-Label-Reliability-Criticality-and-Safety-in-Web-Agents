**Subject:** Question about the §3 critical-path measurement in Prismata (arXiv:2607.08147)

Dear Corban, Alp Eren, Sijun and Professor Popa,

I am Abid Aziz, and I am running a measurement study of structural defenses against prompt injection in web agents, Prismata among them. We are examining the page inputs behind your §3 finding that 1,086 of 90,408 untrusted paths contain an actionable descendant. Our main question concerns the representation of the Mind2Web pages, rather than the critical-path definition.

1. **Mind2Web representation.** Which version of the pages did §3 use: `raw_html` or `cleaned_html` from the Hugging Face release, the MHTML snapshots, or a browser-rendered version? The released `raw_html` keeps 21 attribute names and has no `href`, `onclick`, `tabindex` or `contenteditable` on any of the 57 sites we checked. Requiring an `href` for plain links roughly halves our reconstruction's per-node criticality, so this distinction affects the comparison.
2. **Confirmation of our reading.** We read §3 as counting one path per node flagged untrusted by the provenance model. The Case-3 breakdown identifies plain `a[href]` links and ARIA `role=link` links. Is that the right reading of the unit and link rule across the full measurement? Was actionability checked on stored HTML or using browser metadata?
3. **Per-corpus split.** You report 24,992 untrusted paths from Common Crawl and 65,416 from Mind2Web. How do the 1,086 critical paths and the 94 Case-3 paths divide between those corpora?
4. **Shared inputs.** Could you share the list of the 5,664 DOMs, or your per-node provenance labels for a subset? Even 100 Mind2Web pages would let us compare the measurements on the same pages and labels.

Our work in progress, including the scripts behind these numbers, is public at https://github.com/abidaziz1/Provenance-Label-Reliability-Criticality-and-Safety-in-Web-Agents. We will send you the paper before we submit it, and we would be glad to talk if that is easier than email.
