**Subject:** Reproducing UCM (arXiv:2607.05277) on archived and live pages

Dear Kristina, Egor, Javier, Matthew, Edoardo and Florian,

I am [your name], and I am running a measurement study of structural defenses against prompt injection in web agents. Thank you for releasing the UCM code (ethz-spylab/untrusted-content-masking); we work from commit acff2e4. We have three questions.

1. **Models and settings.** The paper names Claude Sonnet 4.5 for boundary detection (§7.1). Which exact model ID, temperature and commit reproduce Table 2? And which exact agent model IDs match the runs in §5?
2. **Capture dates and saved pages.** When were the Booking, Reddit and GitLab pages behind Table 2 captured? The Booking URLs in `sites/booking/site.py` carry a 1 July 2025 check-in. Could you share the saved HTML (`llm_input_original_*.html`) for Booking, so we can test on exactly your pages?
3. **An observation we would value your view on.**
   - 23 of the 25 hand-written Booking selectors use `data-*` attributes, and so do 74 of the 77 distinct LLM-generated ones.
   - The Mind2Web archive removes all site-authored `data-*` attributes. On 131 archived Booking pages, 24 of the 25 hand selectors match nothing.
   - We captured live Booking pages on 26 Sep 2026 and reduced their attributes to Mind2Web's set. That alone disables 53 of the 57 selectors that match the live pages.

   We read this as a caution about reusing selectors across page representations. We do not read it as a result about UCM on archived pages, since your generator writes selectors from the page it sees. Does that match how you would expect UCM to be evaluated on archived corpora?

Our work in progress, including the scripts behind these numbers, is public at https://github.com/abidaziz1/Provenance-Label-Reliability-Criticality-and-Safety-in-Web-Agents. We will send you the paper before we submit it.
